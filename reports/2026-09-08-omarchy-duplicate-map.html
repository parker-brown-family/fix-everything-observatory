before you trust a band

The grouping is rough and known to be rough: tf‑idf cosine over title and body plus GitHub's link metadata, then banded by patch hunks, declared close references, thread comments and rare shared literals. Neither pass read anything for meaning. Calibrated by reading 83 random pairs in full: a patch duplicating another patch held 13 of 30, a report duplicating another report 15 of 30, a declared Fixes #N 42 of 42, an undeclared one 6 of 18.

Three traps. Same surface is not the same work — nine in ten pairs share a file or a service, only about half are redundant. A shared rare literal proves a shared subsystem, not a shared defect, and it is the evidence behind most of the middle band. And the queue moves: 113 of these 1,072 tickets settled in the 15 hours after the snapshot, so re-fetch state before judging anything.
