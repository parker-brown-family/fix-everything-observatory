WHAT TO TRY

  1. Drag the viewport slider (or the stage's right edge) left. At 80% the strip vanishes and one chunky bar takes the row. Watch the numbers under it cross.
  2. Now put the width back and drag PROJECTS WATCHED instead (14 are available). Same line, crossed from the other side. Measured: a 1400px window keeps three tabs; nine tabs would want a 3,238px one. That is the whole argument for this control.
  3. Press w anywhere — the palette opens in both states, so three-project and fifty-project users keep one muscle memory. / works too.
  4. Type tdel. Subsequence, not substring: it finds terminal-delight. So does t-d, parkbrown and delightbrown (name and owner in one query).
  5. Open with an empty box: SHOWING NOW, then RECENT, then the rest. The switch you actually want is usually one ↓ away.
  6. ↓↑ move and wrap, ↵ opens, Esc closes and puts focus back on the bar, Tab jumps straight to WATCH ANOTHER.
  7. Type nonsense — zzz. Every row filters away; ＋ WATCH ANOTHER stays, because a search that finds nothing is exactly when you want to add one.
  8. JUMP TO 50 PROJECTS: the palette is the same size, the list scrolls, the sticky add-row holds the floor. Then type — fifty rows to one in four keystrokes.
  9. TOGGLE URL MISMATCH: the yellow line welds to the closed bar and rides the palette header — the warning is never on the surface you just left.
  10. Badges survive both states. Narrow the stage hard: the owner truncates, the badge never does.
