The moment a repository starts
healing faster than it breaks —

Every dot is an open bug.

Further out means older.

Since June 2025.
Nine thousand of them.

Violet means a robot filed it.

Grey means no sign.

Hollow means nobody looked.

So it never counts as clean.

Watch the middle band.

Narrowing means it is shrinking.

Half close inside twenty-eight hours.

It is getting quieter.

And this is what you watch for.
